RESPONSIBLE BY DESIGN

Useful intelligence needs
the right guardrails.

AI should be designed with its context, users and risks in mind. We consider data access, human review, evaluation, integration and operational controls as part of solution planning.

01Business contextStart with the use case, users and desired outcome.
02Trusted informationDefine which sources and systems an AI solution can use.
03Human oversightSet review and escalation points for sensitive actions.
04Measure & improveEvaluate outputs and refine the solution over time.
HOW TO BEGIN

Start with one meaningful use case.

You do not need to automate everything at once. Identify a workflow where better information, faster response or reduced manual effort can make a difference.
